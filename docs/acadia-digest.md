## Overview

**Acadia National Park** (park code: `acad`) — Maine, USA.

Acadia National Park protects the natural beauty of the highest rocky headlands along the Atlantic coastline of the United States, an abundance of habitats, and a rich cultural heritage. At 4 million visits a year, it's one of the top 10 most-visited national parks in the United States.

**Headline numbers:**
- 27 miles of historic motor roads
- 158 miles of hiking trails
- 45 miles of carriage roads

- **Website:** https://www.nps.gov/acad/index.htm
- **Contact:** (207) 288-3338 · acadia_information@nps.gov
- **Address:** 25 Visitor Center Road, Hulls Cove Visitor Center, Bar Harbor, ME 04609
- **Weather:** Located on Mount Desert Island, Acadia experiences all four seasons. Fall temperatures range from 30–70°F (−1–21°C). Typically the first frost is in mid-October and the first snowfall begins in November. Pack layers.
- **Entrance fees:** $35 private vehicle (7 days), $30 motorcycle, $20 per person (foot/bike), $6 timed-entry vehicle reservation (location-based, e.g., Cadillac), $70 Acadia Annual Pass.

## Campgrounds (4)

### Blackwoods Campground
- **Location:** East side of Mount Desert Island, ~5 miles south of Bar Harbor on Route 3 (155 Blackwoods Drive, Otter Creek, ME 04660). All sites are wooded and within a 10-minute walk of the ocean.
- **Sites:** 281 total, all reservable — 221 tent-only, 60 RV, 4 group.
- **Fees:** $30/night tent-only non-electric, $30/night RV non-electric, $60/night group tent area.
- **Season:** Usually early May to mid-October.
- **Amenities:** Trash/Recycling Collection; Toilets (Flush Toilets – seasonal); Internet Connectivity; Showers (None); Cell Phone Reception; Laundry; Amphitheater; Dump Station; Camp Store; Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite; Potable Water (Yes – seasonal); Ice Available For Sale; Firewood For Sale; Food Storage Lockers.
- **Accessibility:** 12 accessible drive-in sites; hard-packed walkways to wheelchair-accessible restrooms and amphitheater; RV sites 35-ft max.
- **Reservations:** Recreation.gov — 90% released 6 months ahead, 10% released 14 days out on a rolling basis.

### Seawall Campground
- **Location:** West side of Mount Desert Island, ~4 miles south of Southwest Harbor (664 Seawall Rd, Southwest Harbor, ME 04679); ~18 miles from Bar Harbor. All sites within a short walk of the coastline.
- **Sites:** 202 total, all reservable — 43 tent-only, 59 RV, 98 walk/boat-to, 5 group.
- **Fees:** $30/night standard non-electric, $30/night tent-only non-electric, $30/night RV non-electric, $22/night walk-in, $60/night group tent area.
- **Season:** Typically the Wednesday before Memorial Day to Columbus Day.
- **Amenities:** Trash/Recycling Collection; Toilets (Flush Toilets – seasonal); Internet Connectivity; Showers (None); Cell Phone Reception; Laundry; Amphitheater; Dump Station; Camp Store; Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite; Potable Water (Yes – seasonal); Ice Available For Sale; Firewood For Sale; Food Storage Lockers.
- **Accessibility:** 3 RV accessible sites (35-ft max), 5 drive-in, 5 walk-in, 1 group site; accessible restroom in C loop.
- **Reservations:** Recreation.gov — same release schedule as Blackwoods.

### Schoodic Woods Campground
- **Location:** Schoodic Peninsula, 1.5 miles southeast of Winter Harbor (54 Farview Dr, Winter Harbor, ME 04693); ~60–70 minutes from Bar Harbor.
- **Sites:** 89 total, all reservable — 78 with electrical hookups, 41 RV, 13 tent-only, 9 walk/boat-to, 2 group.
- **Fees:** $22/night hike-in, $30/night standard electric, $30/night tent-only electric, $36/night RV electric no water, $40/night RV electric and water, $60/night group tent.
- **Season:** Wednesday before Memorial Day weekend until Columbus Day.
- **Amenities:** Trash/Recycling Collection; Toilets (Flush Toilets – seasonal, Vault Toilets – seasonal); Internet Connectivity; Showers (None); Cell Phone Reception; Laundry; Amphitheater; Dump Station; Camp Store; Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite; Potable Water (Yes – seasonal); Ice Available For Sale; Firewood For Sale; Food Storage Lockers.
- **Accessibility:** Accessible sites and all restrooms are wheelchair accessible.
- **Reservations:** Recreation.gov.

### Duck Harbor Campground
- **Location:** Isle au Haut, a rugged island off the coast of Stonington, Maine — remote, inaccessible to automobiles, linked to the mainland by mailboat.
- **Sites:** 5 total, all reservable — lean-to shelters; great launch point for paddling excursions.
- **Fees:** $20/night tent/shelter non-electric (max 6 people per site).
- **Regulations:** Reservations required (released 10 am April 1 for the whole season); max 3-night stay, one stay per calendar year; no pets; no generators; quiet hours 10 p.m.–8 a.m.; check-in/check-out 11:00 am; all trash carried out.
- **Amenities:** Trash/Recycling Collection; Toilets (Composting Toilets – seasonal); Internet Connectivity; Showers (None); Cell Phone Reception; Laundry; Amphitheater; Dump Station; Camp Store; Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite; Potable Water (Yes – seasonal); Ice Available For Sale; Firewood For Sale; Food Storage Lockers.
- **Accessibility:** Not wheelchair accessible; rocky, uneven terrain; shelters have a stepped entrance.

## Visitor Centers (6)

### Hulls Cove Visitor Center
- Acadia's main visitor contact station and transportation hub (May into October). Popular and often crowded first stop. Adjacent to an entrance for the 27-mile Park Loop Road and a key stop for the fare-free Island Explorer bus system. 52 steps to the main entrance (separate accessible entrance available).
- **Address:** 25 Visitor Center Road, Bar Harbor, ME 04609
- **Hours:** 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM daily (summer season; first week of May to end of October).

### Sieur de Monts Nature Center
- Visitor services, hiking information, and interactive exhibits; acts as a trailhead for many hiking trails. Rangers present spring through fall.
- **Address:** 47 Sweet Water Circle, Bar Harbor, ME 04609
- **Hours:** 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM daily (June 14 through Oct 12, 2026).

### Acadia Gateway Center
- Intermodal transit and welcome center on Route 3 in Trenton, three miles north of Mount Desert Island. Island Explorer bus stop, day-use parking, welcome center with tourism information, store, waiting area, and restrooms.
- **Address:** Gateway Center Drive, Trenton, ME 04605
- **Hours:** 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM daily (May 20 to Oct 12, 2026).

### Islesford Historical Museum
- Celebrates Islesford's cultural history with exhibits on life on and around the Cranberry Isles from the 1700s to present. Boat access only.
- **Address:** Little Cranberry Island, Islesford, ME 04646
- **Hours:** 10:30 AM – 4:00 PM daily; closes for the season Sep 19, 2026.

### Schoodic Institute Welcome Center at Rockefeller Hall
- Located on Schoodic Peninsula off the one-way loop road. Exhibits about the old Navy base and park information.
- **Address:** 9 Atterbury Circle, Winter Harbor, ME 04693
- **Hours:** 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM, seven days per week.

### Winter Information: Bar Harbor Chamber of Commerce
- During winter and spring (Nov 1 into early May), information and some pass sales move here. Full-day closures on Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year's Eve, and New Year's Day.
- **Address:** 2 Cottage Street, Bar Harbor, ME 04609
- **Hours:** 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM daily.
